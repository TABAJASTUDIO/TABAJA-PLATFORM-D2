AUTO FACE CROP
- One detected face: centred, cropped and scaled automatically.
- No detected face: Smart Center Crop is used.
- Multiple faces: warning is displayed and Smart Center Crop is used for safety.
- The feature uses the browser FaceDetector when available. Other browsers safely fall back to Smart Center Crop.

Always review the preview before a large print job.
