TABAJA NFC BRIDGE - ACR122U

1. Keep the ACR122U connected by USB.
2. Double-click Start-NFC-Bridge.bat.
3. Keep the black PowerShell window open.
4. Open Tabaja Solution > NFC Studio.
5. Put one NFC card on the reader.
6. Enter the website link and press Write Website Link.
7. For 500 cards, enable Auto-write. Remove each completed card and place the next one.

The bridge changes no printer, designer, batch-printing, employee, or template files.
It listens locally on 127.0.0.1:8765 only.
