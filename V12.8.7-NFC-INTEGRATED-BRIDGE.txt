TABaja Solution V12.8.7 — Integrated NFC Bridge

Scope: NFC Studio and NFC-Bridge only. No Card Designer, Batch Printing, Zebra, template, employee, or print-engine files were changed.

Changes:
- NFC Studio connects to the local writer through secure window messaging, avoiding Edge loopback blocking.
- Bridge status, reader status, card UID, Read, Write, Verify and Auto-write are reflected inside NFC Studio.
- Bridge uses port 8766.
- URL verification normalizes protocol, www and trailing slash differences.
- Write verification retries before reporting a mismatch.

Use:
1. Run NFC-Bridge\Start-NFC-Bridge.bat.
2. Open NFC Studio.
3. Press Connect NFC Bridge once. Keep the small local writer window open or minimized.
4. Work from NFC Studio.
