TABAJA CARD DESIGNER V7.1 - QUICK TEST

1) Open index.html and login.
2) Upload Excel.
3) Select Employee Photos folder. Name photos by Employee ID: 000001.jpg, 000002.jpg.
4) Select Company Logos folder. Name each logo by company: B7S.png.
5) In Excel Company column use B7S (or use a Logo Filename column containing B7S.png).
6) Click Preview Record.
7) Test records 1 to 2 before printing 50.

EXAMPLE
Employee ID | Full Name | Company | Logo Filename
000001 | John Kamara | B7S | B7S.png
000002 | Ibrahim Sesay | B7S | B7S.png

Logo Filename is optional when Company exactly matches the logo filename.
